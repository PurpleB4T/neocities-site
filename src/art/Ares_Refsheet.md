Refsheet I made for **Artfight 2026**.

This was the first refsheet I made this year and probably is also my favourite. Ares didn't really have a proper sheet before so he was long overdue.\
I had also redesigned his tattoos with a friend around a month or two before artfight. We simplified the original design (so its easier to replicate quickly), while also making it feel more, idk, him!\
The portraits I actually drew because I started solo-playing *daggerheart* and I needed token art for all the characters!
