<h3> Artist Comments </h3>

Refsheet I did for Slinky for **Artfight 2026**.

Just a coompilation of clothes he successfully stole from various people. All matched into *stunning* outfits!

I originally drew this back in early 2025 when I first made up Slinky. It was really just some rough sketches but I had a lot of fun with them.\
Now that I have decided for a proper time period in which the *SLINKYVERSE* is set I referenced fashion from the era when redoing this sheet.\
There is probably a lot to improve (also I am not as good at drawing clothes as I'd like to be but oh well...) as finding good and accurate references for historical clothing is hard... and now with a bunch of AI images flooding everything it's even harder...\
If you know of any good places to find historical fashion references (around 18th, 19th and early 20th century especially), I would love to know!
