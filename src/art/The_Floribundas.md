<h3> Artist Comments </h3>

Some time around late winter to early spring a friend and I decided we wanted to potentially play an AU of each of our characters in *Pathfinder 2e*.\
And because the *Druid* and the *Commander* classes are so up my ally, I decided to revamp my oldest *Eos AU*: **Mentally StablEos**! (he is a druid)

Sadly, as things go, we haven't really managed anything but the setup so far, with both of us starting new jobs and being faily busy (as well as some TTRPG burnout for both of us).\
*BUT* I gave these two shiny new redesigns which really took a practically dead AU and turned them into some of my faves!

Once I actually make an *OC wiki* I will fully go in depth on them, but for now what you need to know is that in this AU Eos basically just accepts himself instead of running away from his gay thoughts. And he turns a lot happier for it (who could have thunk).\
(I also haven't really shared a lot about Eos either... so discussing AUs is extra hard lol).

The AU is either called **Mentally StablEos** (because Eos goes to therapy earlier) or **The Floribundas** (as their clan name in this is Floribunda).\
*Eos* is a druid (of the Leaf Order) and *Ares* is a Commander. Their little companion is Eos' familiar (in lore a dryad, in mechanics of PF2e a Leshy) called *Twiggy*.\
They are all *wisteria* themed as their clan tree is a wisteria (and Twiggy is the driad born from this tree).
