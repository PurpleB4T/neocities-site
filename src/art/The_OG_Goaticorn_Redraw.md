<h3> Artist Comments </h3>

I originally drew this *late 2023* while I lived in Japan and printed it at a 711.\
And because I liked it so much and I tried to post art on socials he became my *profile picture* for most platforms.

Then I looked at the full version again and I found it looked a bit boring and flat... so I redid the lineart and recoloured it.
