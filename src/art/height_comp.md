<h3> Artist Comments </h3>

Height Comparison I made for **Artfight 2026**.

Featuring **Eos**, **Ares** and both their parents each.\
I'm really proud of this one!

**From left to right:**
- *Maro*: he is Eos'  father, and the head druid of the local satyr clans. He is based on a spotted pygmy goat and John Lennon. 
- *Eos*: half unicorn, half satyr. Tall for a pygmy goat, short for a unicorn.
- *Ares*: Eos' best friend and partner (handfastened for many years). Half satyr, half bicorn. Short for an angus, tall for a bicorn
- *Zoe*: she is Ares' mother and due to being exluded from hornings\* made her own fighting league for only hornless satyrs. Absolute menace and very anti authority. She is based on Angus cows
- *Tia*: Ares' birthmother. Bicorn, fell in love with Zoe and now lives with satyrs. A bit of an outcast, but despite her being a bicorn, much more reserved and held back.
- *Estar*: Eos' mother. Fairly rebellious, always hated living with unicorns. Came from a prestigious family and was to be married off to a man she hated. Fell in love with a druid instead.

For the fact that unicorns and bicorns are more animal shaped then human, don't think about it too much...\
I'm not sure if they have the capacity of turning into humanoids temporarily (like in *The Last Unicorn*) or smth... but it is a part I choose to ignore.\
Both Eos and Ares were originally joke characters so I never really thought of the logistics of it too much and I still avoid it for... obvious reasons.\
Unicorns and Bicorns are fully sentient however!


Also unicorns are fucking massive...

\* **HORNING**: fighting tournaments in which satyrs bud heads using their horns.
