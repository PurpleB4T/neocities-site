# About
Hi!\
I'm **lunacytie** (sometimes spooks).\ 
I changed by username so many times, most of the time a variant of my very first one, named after my very first Sonic OC, called Luna. This one is hopefully my final iteration. 

I always lurked on social media, and never really posted my own work. It felt very intimidating.\

I also enjoy *worldbuilding*, *TTRPGs* and *video games*. I also started reading more again recently.
